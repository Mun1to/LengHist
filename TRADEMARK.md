Everything else here is yours to take. This is the one line that does not move.

## Not allowed

- Publishing a fork, mirror, rebuild, app, extension or service **under the name Vibeset**.
- Registering domains built on the name: `vibeset.xyz`, `vibeset.io`, `vibeset.app`,
  `getvibeset.com`, `vibeset.es` and the like, including misspellings and near-copies
  (`vibesets`, `vibe-set`, `viberset`) close enough to be mistaken for this project.
- Using the logo or the icons as the identity of your project, product, service or account.
- Opening accounts on GitHub, npm, app stores or social platforms under that name.
- Implying affiliation, endorsement or sponsorship: no "official", no "powered by Vibeset".

## Always allowed, no permission needed

- Naming Vibeset to refer to Vibeset: linking, quoting, reviewing, teaching, comparing, criticising.
- Saying where something came from: "component taken from Vibeset", "based on Vibeset".

## If you fork it

Rename the project, remove the assets in `redesign/public/brand/`, and credit the original with a
visible link to <https://vibeset.dev>. The credit is a licence term, not a courtesy: see
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

Questions about a specific use: open an issue and ask.
